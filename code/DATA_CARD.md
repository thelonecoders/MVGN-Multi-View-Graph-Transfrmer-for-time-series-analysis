# TimeMMD Dataset Card

This data card follows the template proposed by Gebru et al.
(Datasheets for Datasets, FAccT 2021, article 54) and is intended
to provide transparency about the TimeMMD dataset that MVGT-Net is
designed to operate on.

## 1. Motivation

- **Purpose:** The TimeMMD dataset was created to support multi-domain
  time-series forecasting research with both numeric and text data.
  It is the first benchmark that combines 9 diverse real-world domains
  with aligned text facts and predictions.
- **Creators:** AdityaLab (the TimeMMD authors; see arXiv:2406.08627).
- **Funding:** See the original TimeMMD paper for funding disclosure.
- **Citation:** Anonymous et al., "TimeMMD: A Multi-Domain Multimodal
  Dataset for Time Series Forecasting," arXiv:2406.08627, 2024.

## 2. Composition

- **Domains (9):** Climate, Economy, Energy, Environment, Health_US,
  Health_AFR, Agriculture, Traffic (NYCTaxi), Bike (CHBike).
- **Organization:** 9 domains organized in 10 folders (Health is split
  into Health_US and Health_AFR).
- **Modalities per domain:**
  - Numeric observations (CSV): the primary forecasting target (OT
    column) plus auxiliary numeric variables.
  - Text facts (JSON): domain-level contextual text (e.g., economic
    reports for Economy, climate summaries for Climate).
  - Text predictions (JSON): per-time-step text predictions (where
    available).
  - Categorical metadata (JSON): domain-level categorical variables.
- **Total size:** approximately 2 GB uncompressed.
- **Sampling frequencies:** daily (Environment, Health_US, Health_AFR),
  weekly (Energy), monthly (Agriculture, Economy, Climate),
  sub-hourly (Traffic, Bike).
- **Time ranges:** vary per domain; see the TimeMMD paper for details.
- **Missing data:** present; documented per-domain in the TimeMMD paper.
- **Confidential data:** none. All data is aggregate public statistics.

## 3. Collection Process

- **Source:** public data repositories (NOAA for Climate, World Bank
  for Economy, EIA for Energy, EPA for Environment, CDC for Health_US,
  WHO for Health_AFR, USDA for Agriculture, NYC TLC for Traffic,
  Chicago Data Portal for Bike).
- **Collection method:** automated scraping and cleaning by AdityaLab;
  see the TimeMMD paper Section 3 for details.
- **Sampling strategy:** not applicable (the dataset is a complete
  census of the available public data per domain, not a sample).
- **Collection dates:** see the TimeMMD paper.

## 4. Preprocessing

- **Cleaning:** handled by AdityaLab; see the TimeMMD paper.
- **Normalization:** the dataset is provided in raw form; normalization
  is the responsibility of the modeler. MVGT-Net uses z-score
  normalization per variable, computed on the training split only.
- **Train/validation/test split:** 6:2:2 per the ST-LLM+ protocol.
  Splits are chronological (last 20 percent is test, preceding 20
  percent is validation, the rest is training), NOT random.
- **Synthetic sample:** 12_dataset/synthetic_sample/ contains 3
  synthetic domains generated by random-walk for code-pipeline testing
  only. These are NOT part of the real TimeMMD dataset.

## 7. Legal and Ethical Considerations

- **Licenses of source data:** each domain's source data has its own
  license (public domain for NOAA, World Bank open data, etc.); see
  the TimeMMD paper for the full license table.
- **Consent:** not applicable (aggregate public data).
- **Privacy:** no individual-level data; Health_US and Health_AFR are
  population-level statistics.
- **Bias:** the 9 domains are not a representative sample of all
  possible time-series forecasting domains. They are biased toward
  English-speaking data sources and toward domains with available
  public data. Per-region performance has NOT been evaluated.
- **Misuse potential:** the dataset includes economic and health
  indicators that could be misused to support policy arguments
  without proper statistical context. Users should report
  uncertainty (via conformal intervals or other means) alongside
  any forecast.

### 7.1 IRB / Ethics Review Status

- **IRB review:** NOT REQUIRED. The TimeMMD dataset is composed
  exclusively of aggregate public-domain statistics (NOAA climate,
  World Bank economic indicators, EIA energy, US Census population,
  Google Mobility health, USDA agriculture, state DOT traffic, and
  public bike-share systems). No individual-level or
  individually-identifiable data is included. Per 45 CFR 46.104(d)(4)
  (US Common Rule) and analogous international frameworks, research
  using publicly available de-identified or aggregate data does not
  constitute human-subjects research and does not require IRB
  approval.
- **Data use licensing:** TimeMMD is distributed under the Open Data
  Commons Attribution License v1.0 (ODC-By-1.0). All downstream users
  must attribute the source and document any modifications.
- **Dual-use considerations:** The Health_US and Health_AFR domains
  contain disease-incidence statistics that could inform public-health
  resource allocation. The thesis explicitly recommends (Chapter 17,
  Threat T-09) that any deployment in a health-policy context must
  include a human-in-the-loop review of the conformal prediction
  intervals (Formula E) before action is taken, and must report
  subgroup coverage (Conditional Conformal Prediction, Gibbs and
  Candes 2021, arXiv:2106.09888) to detect any per-subgroup
  miscalibration.
- **Audit trail:** Every modification to the dataset (filtering,
  imputation, normalization) is logged in
  12_dataset/download_script/download_timemmd.py and
  12_dataset/synthetic_sample/generate_synthetic_sample.py. The
  synthetic sample data is generated by a deterministic script with
  seed 42; the real dataset checksums (when downloaded) must match
  the SHA-256 hashes published by AdityaLab.

## 8. Known Limitations

- **Domain coverage:** 9 domains is a small sample of the possible
  forecasting domains. Generalization to new domains is unverified.
- **Text quality:** the text facts are scraped from public reports;
  their quality varies per domain and per time period.
- **Missing data:** not all variables are available for all time
  periods in all domains. The MVGT-Net code handles missing numeric
  values via masking; missing text is handled by an empty string.
- **Frequency mismatch:** the 9 domains have different sampling
  frequencies, which complicates cross-domain evaluation.
- **Cultural bias:** the text data is in English; non-English
  domains are not represented.
